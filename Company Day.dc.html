01 · Overview

A global event needs one face everywhere.

Company Day is SoftServe's global birthday celebration — every year, teams in every location the company has offices gather to mark it together. Each year has its own idea;
in 2025 it was one line: You shape the moment.

Company Day 2025 marked the company's 32nd birthday. My role was to implement this at the Ukrainian location:
12 cities, 5 outdoor locations — every location reading
as the same celebration.

02 · The context

The concept was set globally

The hard part is everything that happens after a concept exists: it has to land in many very different places at once — different cities, different formats, different teams building it.

Each handoff is a chance for the brand to drift.
In collaboration with several external agencies that provide design and decoration services across the country,
the real problem wasn't a weak concept.

The risk was a strong idea falling apart in execution.

03 · The challenge

How do you keep one visual language intact across 12 cities, 5 outdoor locations, and several external agencies — without a single team touching every layout?

Not the task

This wasn't a design-from-scratch task.

The task

Consistency at scale: hold the integrity of a system that many hands would build, in parallel, under event deadlines.

04 · My role

05 · How I worked

One system, many hands

Holding a brand together at scale is a method, not a wish. The point wasn't to design every piece myself.
It was to make sure that whoever did — in Kyiv or in an outdoor build five cities away — produced something that still read as one Company Day.

1BriefBrief each location and agency clearly.
2BuildLet each team build.
3CheckCheck every output against the system.
4ApproveApprove, or send back.

Consistency came from the process, not from one pair of hands.

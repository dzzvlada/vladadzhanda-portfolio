I’m a brand strategist and designer with 6+ years building brands that hold together — from the positioning that defines them to the visual system that carries it.

My work sits where most brands break: between what a business wants and what its audience actually feels. Three years in employer branding taught me that gap is impossible to fake — people either recognise themselves in a brand, or they don’t.

I start with research, not references: audience first, business goal second, visual last. It’s slower — and it’s why the brands I build stay consistent long after I’ve handed them over.
